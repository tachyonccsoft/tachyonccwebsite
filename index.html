Tachyon CC

A free forever Contact Center software


Operate easily your Contact Center with Tachyon, start dialing a campaign in less than 5 minutes

  • Outbound Campaigns Dialer
    • Supported modes: Progressive,Power Dial, Predictive
  • Inbound Campaigns mode
  • Agent blending capabilities
  • Easy integrated scripting tool for Agents
  • Schedule and Capture system for outbound calls
  • Robust PBX under the hood, MikoPBX based on Asterisk

⚠️ Tachyon CC is under development, is not suited for production enviroments
